# 2026 Sri Lankan Vesak source verification

Verified on 2026-11-05.

2026 has a documented **civil holiday versus State Vesak observance distinction**.

- May 1, 2026: Vesak Full Moon Poya Day in the official Sri Lankan public/bank holiday calendar (Central Bank).
- May 30, 2026: State Vesak religious observance day for Sri Buddha Varsha 2570, according to Department of Buddhist Affairs, within State Vesak Week May 27–June 2. The government holiday calendar continues to label May 30 Adhi Poson.
- The State Vesak celebration date does not amend the gazetted holiday schedule.

The SDK retains its May 30 observance reference in 2026; it uses 2026-05-29T18:30:00Z (May 30 midnight in Colombo) for display rollover. This midnight is a PROJECT CONVENTION, NOT a verified historic/religious new-year transition instant. The code exposes boundaryVerified=false to document that limit, while verified=true refers solely to the source-backed BE 2570 label associated with the 2026 State Vesak event. Other years remain provisional.

Astronomical full-moon instants, government Poya holidays, and chosen religious observance days are distinct. Never substitute this project for an official religious calendar.
