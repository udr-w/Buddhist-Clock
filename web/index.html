04 / THE MODEL

A new coordinate, not a new physical second.

An average full-moon cycle lasts approximately 29.53 conventional days. Normalizing it into 28 days makes a virtual second longer than an SI second. Phase-tracking seconds change duration as the astronomical lunar phase advances.

The Moon is Earth's nearest major celestial neighbor and contributes to tides. But the Moon is not a “solar component,” and its gravity is not what causes the mapped second to lengthen. This is a deliberately designed calendar.

This clock is inspired by Buddhist lunar ideas, not an official Buddhist lunisolar calendar or Sri Lankan Poya calendar. The 2026 State Vesak Buddhist Era transition is curated; other years are provisional. Keep UTC/monotonic clocks for real-time expiry, scheduling, financial transactions and security.
